It's Full of Sparks

A platform-adventure game whose hero is a firecracker with a lit fuse: you have to reach the end of each level before it explodes. Built in Unity for iOS and Android; published by Noodlecake Studios at launch and later moved to Dabadu Games.

Highlights

  • +50,000 downloads on day one on the App Store.
  • Featured by Apple in two editorial collections (April 2018): "Juegos de fuego y hielo" and "Juegos ardientes".
  • Translated into 31 languages.
  • +80 levels across 4 worlds (Suburbs, Desert, Cave and Great North).
  • Worked on the final stretch of development and, later on, moved the game from Noodlecake's stores to Dabadu Games with a maintenance update (v2.2.1, 2023).
